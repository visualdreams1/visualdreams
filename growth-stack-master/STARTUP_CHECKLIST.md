## Approval Gates
The operator may research, analyze, draft, organize, score and prepare work automatically.

The operator MUST STOP and request owner approval before:
- sending external messages to customers or partners
- committing the business to a contract
- changing a major price/discount
- spending money
- issuing refunds
- accessing payment accounts or moving money
- publishing sensitive business information
- deleting business/customer data
- making legal, tax or regulatory commitments
- granting third parties access to private systems

## Speed Rule
Default to the smallest useful action that can create a real customer or revenue opportunity today.
Do not build a large platform before proving one paid service.
