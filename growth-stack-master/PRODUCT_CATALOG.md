# Growth Stack Global — Product Catalog v0.1

## P01 — WhatsApp AI Employee
Customer problem: repetitive WhatsApp questions, missed leads, slow replies.
Outcome: 24/7 automated first-line support and lead capture.
Initial target: local SMEs, hospitality, retail, salons, tour operators.
Status: DEMO TO BUILD

## P02 — AI Customer Support Agent
Customer problem: repetitive support work across web/email/chat.
Outcome: faster responses and escalation to humans when needed.
Status: CONCEPT

## P03 — AI Sales Agent
Customer problem: leads are not followed up consistently.
Outcome: qualification, follow-up and pipeline organization.
Status: CONCEPT

## P04 — AI Voice Receptionist
Customer problem: missed calls and appointment requests.
Outcome: automated call answering, qualification, scheduling and handoff.
Status: CONCEPT

## P05 — AI Business Automation
Customer problem: repetitive admin workflows.
Outcome: connected workflows that reduce manual work.
Status: CONCEPT

## P06 — AI Marketing Machine
Customer problem: inconsistent marketing production.
Outcome: repeatable content and campaign production system.
Status: CONCEPT

## P07 — AI Research & Intelligence
Customer problem: expensive/time-consuming market and competitor research.
Outcome: concise decision-ready research reports.
Status: READY FOR VALIDATION

## P08 — AI Proposal & Funding Desk
Customer problem: businesses and organizations struggle to produce strong proposals.
Outcome: professional proposals, plans, decks and reports.
Status: READY FOR VALIDATION

## P09 — AI Implementation Service
Customer problem: businesses do not know where or how to deploy AI.
Outcome: identify high-value workflows and implement practical systems.
Status: CONCEPT

## Product rule
Do not add a new product merely because it is technically interesting. Validate customer demand first. Prefer products that can become repeatable and recurring.
