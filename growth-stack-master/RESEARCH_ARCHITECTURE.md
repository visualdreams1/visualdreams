# Growth Stack Global — Worldwide Research Architecture v0.2

## Mission
Continuously discover, verify, score and prioritize AI-service opportunities worldwide, while keeping commercial and financial actions behind Dennis's approval gate.

## Research loop
1. Generate broad opportunity hypotheses.
2. Search multiple markets and sectors.
3. Collect current evidence: demand, buyer pain, competitors, pricing, job/project signals, product launches, regulations and adoption.
4. Cross-check important claims with multiple sources.
5. Normalize evidence into structured opportunity records.
6. Score each opportunity.
7. Detect duplicates and weak evidence.
8. Rank by speed-to-revenue and long-term value.
9. Produce a short list for owner approval.
10. Turn approved opportunities into demos, offers and prospect lists.
11. Re-run research and update scores as the market changes.

## Worldwide coverage
Markets are not limited to Kenya. The engine is designed to scan:
- Africa and African diaspora markets
- North America
- Latin America and Caribbean
- Europe
- Middle East
- South Asia
- Southeast Asia
- East Asia
- Oceania

## Evidence priority
Prefer:
1. official government/regulator/company sources
2. primary research and reputable industry research
3. current business/news reporting
4. job/project/marketplace signals
5. community discussions as supporting evidence only

## Opportunity score
Default dimensions:
- urgent buyer pain
- current demand evidence
- ability to pay
- speed to first sale
- low startup cost
- ease of building a convincing demo
- recurring revenue potential
- competition intensity
- customer accessibility
- geographic portability
- fit with Growth Stack capabilities
- regulatory/operational risk

## Anti-hype rules
- Never call an opportunity "hot" solely because an article says so.
- Separate evidence from inference.
- Record dates for time-sensitive evidence.
- Identify contradictory evidence.
- Do not fabricate customer names, prices, market sizes or demand.
- A market trend is not a customer.
- The final test is willingness to pay.

## Action boundary
Research may run automatically. Contacting prospects, spending money, entering contracts, changing material prices, handling payment accounts or publishing sensitive information requires owner approval.
