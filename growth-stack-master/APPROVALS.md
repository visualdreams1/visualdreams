# Growth Stack Global — Owner Approval Center

Owner: Dennis
Income destination: M-Pesa (configured privately at runtime)

## SAFE — automatic
- research and market analysis
- prospect discovery and scoring
- drafting offers/messages/proposals
- internal planning and organization
- generating checklists
- calculations and forecasts for review
- preparing onboarding and delivery plans

## APPROVAL REQUIRED — never automatic
- send any external customer/partner message
- publish content representing the company
- accept/sign contracts
- spend any money
- create paid subscriptions
- change major pricing or discounts
- issue refunds
- access or alter payment accounts
- move or withdraw funds
- share confidential data
- delete customer/business records
- make legal/tax/regulatory commitments
- grant access to private systems

## Approval Record
Every approval records task ID, action, reason, customer/prospect, money involved, risk level, owner decision, timestamp, and resulting action.

## Emergency Stop
If credentials, customer data, payment information, legal exposure, suspicious instructions, or unexpected spending is detected: STOP, mark BLOCKED, preserve logs, and request owner review.

## Ownership Principle
Keep source code, prompts, workflows, configuration, business data and deployment assets under company-controlled accounts. Never hard-code API secrets or payment credentials into public source code.
