# Mission 001 — First Customer

## Objective
Move Growth Stack Global from planning to a validated, sellable AI service and pursue the first paying customer with minimal cash.

## Day 1 — Position
- Choose one initial vertical.
- Define one painful business problem.
- Define one AI solution.
- Define a simple offer with a clear outcome.

## Day 2 — Demonstration
- Build a lightweight demonstration.
- Prepare screenshots/video or a live walkthrough.
- Document what is real versus simulated.

## Day 3 — Prospecting
- Build a list of 20–30 qualified prospects.
- Record business, contact channel, likely problem and next action.
- Prioritize prospects by fit and accessibility.

## Day 4 — Outreach
- Prepare personalized messages.
- Send only approved outreach.
- Track responses and objections.

## Day 5 — Discovery
- Speak with interested prospects.
- Identify their current workflow, pain, cost and desired outcome.
- Do not sell a solution before understanding the problem.

## Day 6 — Proposal
- Create a simple outcome-based proposal.
- Offer a small pilot where appropriate.
- Ask for payment or a clearly defined paid pilot.

## Day 7 — Review
- Count prospects, replies, calls, proposals and revenue.
- Record objections and lessons.
- Decide whether to improve, change the niche, or scale the offer.

## Success condition
A real customer pays for a real service, even if the first transaction is small.

## Failure condition
Do not interpret zero sales after seven days as proof that AI services do not work. Diagnose the offer, niche, proof, outreach volume, trust and pricing before changing direction.
